1. ELEVATOR PITCH (30-SECOND INTRODUCTION)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"ApplyWise is a Proof-First AI Career Readiness and Skill Gap Analysis platform.
Most job seekers and students apply to roles blindly with generic résumés, not
knowing which exact skills they are missing or how to prove the skills they
claim to have. ApplyWise solves this by acting as an Evidence-Driven Career
Agent: instead of just listing keywords, it links every claimed skill to
verifiable proof (GitHub repositories, certificates, project files, and URLs),
calculates an objective Role Readiness Score, builds an interactive 4-Phase
Career Roadmap and 7-Day Proof Plan with curated YouTube/Udemy/Coursera courses,
identifies low-friction Career Pivots, tailors résumés to specific job
descriptions, and tracks applications end-to-end."


2. PROBLEM STATEMENT (WHY THIS PROJECT EXISTS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Keyword Inflation vs. Real Proof:
   Candidates list dozens of skills on their résumé without evidence, leading
   to rejections during technical screening.
2. Blind Job Applications:
   Students don't know their quantitative readiness score for a specific job
   posting (e.g., Google, Microsoft, Oracle, Zoho) before applying.
3. Static Skill Gap Advice:
   Existing tools only tell you what skills you are missing—they don't give you
   an actionable 7-Day Sprint Plan, a 4-Phase Career Roadmap, or direct links
   to learn those skills on YouTube, Udemy, and Coursera.
4. Unclear Career Pivot Pathways:
   When a candidate isn't ready for their primary target role, they don't know
   which adjacent IT roles (e.g., pivoting from Frontend to Full Stack or QA
   Automation) best leverage their existing transferable skills.


3. CORE FEATURES & MODULES (WHAT WE BUILT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Workspace Overview (Command Center)
   - Displays a personalized welcome banner with the user's active Target Role.
   - Real-time KPI summary cards: Verified Skill Proofs, Uploaded Attachments,
     Saved Job Matches, and Active Applications.
   - Quick-navigation shortcuts to jump directly into Skill Readiness,
     Interactive Career Roadmap, Attachments, or Real Company Job Offers.

B. Skill Readiness & Proof Engine ("Skill Readiness" Tab)
   - Interactive Role Readiness Score Gauge:
     Calculates a real-time percentage score (bounded 5%–98%) based on matched
     skills, proof-verified skills (+4% bonus per verified artifact), and
     7-day sprint task completion.
   - IT Job Role Selector (10 Curated Career Tracks):
     Includes Software Engineer, Frontend React & TypeScript Engineer, Backend
     Java & Spring Boot Engineer, Full Stack Developer, Data Engineer & SQL
     Architect, Cloud & DevOps Engineer, AI & Machine Learning Engineer,
     Cybersecurity & SecOps Analyst, QA Automation & SDET Engineer, and Site
     Reliability Engineer (SRE).
   - Skill Evidence Matrix:
     Categorizes every skill as "Verified" (backed by a certificate, repo, or
     document) or "Claimed" (needs proof). Users can attach URLs or uploaded
     files directly to any skill.
   - Interactive 7-Day Proof Sprint Plan:
     Generates a day-by-day action plan to close top skill gaps, complete with
     checkable tasks and direct learning links to YouTube, Udemy, and Coursera.

C. Interactive Career Roadmap & Pivot Engine ("Career Roadmap" Tab)
   - 4-Phase Progression Map:
     * Phase 1 (Weeks 1–2): Core Foundations & Verified Baseline
     * Phase 2 (Weeks 3–6): Closing Critical Skill Gaps
     * Phase 3 (Weeks 7–9): Production Proof & Architecture Verification
     * Phase 4 (Weeks 10–12): Target Role Interview & Offer Readiness
   - Interactive Milestone Tracking & Role Simulator:
     Users can check/uncheck milestones to see their Roadmap Progress % and
     Projected Readiness % update in real time, or simulate how their skills
     map to any of the 10 IT Career Tracks or saved Job Matches.
   - Adjacent Career Pivot Explorer:
     Analyzes the user's current skill foundation and ranks alternative career
     paths into "Low Friction Pivot", "Strategic Adjacent Pivot", and "Growth
     Stretch Pivot". Shows Transferable Skills vs. Bridge Skills to Learn,
     estimated transition weeks, and real hiring companies.

D. Attachments & Document Vault ("Attachments" Tab)
   - Drag-and-drop file upload supporting PDF, DOCX, TXT, MD, JSON, CSV, PNG,
     and JPG (up to 10MB) stored via Multer & SQLite metadata.
   - Categorizes uploads into Résumé / CV, Certificate, Project Proof, or Other.
   - One-click "Extract Text & Analyze Skills" parses uploaded documents to
     automatically detect technical skills and add them to the user's profile.

E. AI Job Match Analyzer ("Job Match" Tab)
   - Paste any job description (or load one directly from the Job Offers board)
     to run a deep comparison against the user's profile and verified evidence.
   - Outputs: Overall Match Score %, Matched Skills, Missing/Gap Skills,
     Recommended Proof Items to highlight, and a 7-Day Action Plan tailored to
     that specific job posting.

F. Live & Curated Tier-1 Company Job Offers ("Job Offers" Tab)
   - Features 12+ real-world technology job offers from leading companies:
     Google, Microsoft, Oracle, Zoho, Amazon, TCS, Infosys, Cisco, Salesforce,
     Adobe, IBM, and Flipkart, plus live external job search integration.
   - Shows real-time Skill Demand Trends across the job market.
   - Users can click "Analyze Readiness" on any job card to immediately load
     that role into the Job Match engine, or apply directly on LinkedIn/Naukri.

G. Résumé Studio ("Résumé Studio" Tab)
   - Live résumé editor and formatter that pulls in the user's verified skills,
     projects, education, and experience.
   - Includes AI-assisted bullet improvement and tailoring for target roles,
     plus clean print/PDF export.

H. Application Tracker ("Applications" Tab)
   - Kanban / status tracker for managing job applications across stages:
     Saved, Applied, Interviewing, Offer, and Rejected, with notes and links.


4. SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & TECH STACK (FOR TECHNICAL QUESTIONS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Frontend:
  * React 19 + TypeScript + Vite
  * Tailwind CSS for responsive, dark/light theme styling
  * Lucide React for iconography
- Backend:
  * Node.js + Express (`server.ts`) running as a unified full-stack server
  * Multer for multi-format file uploads (`uploads/` directory)
- Database & Persistence:
  * SQLite via `better-sqlite3` (`server/db.ts`) for fast, zero-configuration
    relational persistence.
  * Tables: `users`, `sessions`, `profiles`, `skills`, `evidence`,
    `job_analyses`, `applications`, and `uploads`.
- AI & Multi-Provider Inference Engine (Nebius x NVIDIA Hackathon Architecture):
  * NVIDIA NIM API (`https://integrate.api.nvidia.com/v1/chat/completions`) via
    `NVIDIA_API_KEY`, powered by `meta/llama-3.3-70b-instruct` (primary) and
    `nvidia/llama-3.1-nemotron-70b-instruct` (fallback).
  * Nebius AI Studio API (`https://api.studio.nebius.ai/v1/chat/completions`) via
    `NEBIUS_API_KEY`, running `meta-llama/Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct` and
    `nvidia/Llama-3_1-Nemotron-70B-Instruct-HF`.
  * Google Gemini API (`@google/genai` SDK in `server/analyzer.ts`) as secondary
    cloud fallback.
  * Deterministic Fallback & Skill Matcher Engine (`src/utils/skillMatcher.ts`):
    Ensures 100% reliability and instant calculations for skill extraction,
    readiness scoring, 7-day sprint generation, 4-phase roadmaps, and career
    pivot ranking even if offline or without external API latency.

6. COMMON Q&A / VIVA QUESTIONS & ANSWERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q1: How is the Role Readiness Score calculated?
A1: It combines three weighted factors:
    (1) The ratio of matched target-role skills to total required skills (up to
        78 base points),
    (2) A verification bonus (+4% per skill backed by concrete evidence in the
        Evidence Vault, up to +16 points), and
    (3) Progress on the 7-Day Proof Plan (+10 points scaled by completed tasks),
    clamped between 5% and 98% for realism.

Q2: How does the Career Pivot Engine decide which roles are "Low Friction"?
A2: For every role in our 10-track IT catalog, the engine compares the role's
    core skill requirements against the user's current + verified skills.
    - If >=55% of skills transfer (or <=3 bridge skills are needed), it is
      classified as a "Low Friction Pivot" (~3–6 weeks transition).
    - If >=35% of skills transfer (or <=5 bridge skills are needed), it is a
      "Strategic Adjacent Pivot" (~6–10 weeks).
    - Otherwise, it is a "Growth Stretch Pivot".

Q3: What happens if the external AI service is slow or unavailable during a demo?
A3: We built a hybrid architecture (`server/analyzer.ts` + `src/utils/skillMatcher.ts`).
    Alongside the Gemini AI analyzer, we have a deterministic skill-matching,
    roadmap, and 7-day sprint engine tested with automated unit tests so the
    application never crashes or returns empty results.

7. HOW TAVILY IS USED AT RUNTIME (REAL CALL SITES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ApplyWise integrates live web queries and site content extraction via Tavily
(`server/tavily.ts`) across 4 key runtime touchpoints:

1. Live Job Offers Search (GET /api/jobs):
   Queries active 2026 hiring postings across LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed, and
   Wellfound. Deduplicates results and displays them above featured enterprise
   roles with a "Live web results via Tavily" badge and direct source links.

2. Blocked Job Description Extraction (POST /api/jd-fetch-url):
   When a target job URL is protected by Cloudflare/CORS, login walls, or
   requires client-side JavaScript execution, ApplyWise transparently falls
   back to `tavilyExtract([url])` to retrieve the clean raw job description.

3. AI Career Copilot Live Web Context (POST /api/ai/chat):
   When users ask about market salaries, hiring trends, 2026 tools, or toggle
   "Search web (Tavily)", Tavily performs a live search. Results and summary
   answers are injected into the system prompt, routed to the Ultra reasoning
   model (Nemotron Ultra), and displayed with interactive source chips.

4. Skill Readiness 2026 Market Demand (POST /api/skill-demand):
   Under the Skill Readiness missing skills table, candidates can click
   "Market demand" to fetch 2026 employer expectations and hiring trends via
   Tavily, summarized into exactly two concise sentences by the Nebius Fast model.
